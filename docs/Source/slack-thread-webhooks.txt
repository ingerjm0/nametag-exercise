[2026-02-20 09:41] Priya Raghavan
heads up for anyone fielding webhook questions after the v2 rollout — the signature scheme changed too and I don't think it made the release notes

[2026-02-20 09:42] Priya Raghavan
old: X-Nametag-Signature, HMAC-SHA1 over the raw body
new: Nametag-Signature, HMAC-SHA256 over `{timestamp}.{raw_body}`, hex encoded
and we send the timestamp separately in a Nametag-Timestamp header (unix seconds)

[2026-02-20 09:43] Priya Raghavan
so the string you sign is literally the timestamp, then a period, then the raw body bytes. that's it.

[2026-02-20 09:47] Dan Osei
wait we dropped SHA1 entirely?

[2026-02-20 09:48] Priya Raghavan
yes. SHA1 signing was flagged in the Q4 security review. v2 endpoints are SHA256 only.

[2026-02-20 09:49] Dan Osei
what happens to everyone on the old header

[2026-02-20 09:52] Priya Raghavan
we're dual-sending during the migration window. both X-Nametag-Signature (sha1, body only) and Nametag-Signature (sha256, timestamp.body) go out on every delivery. old header comes off on 2026-06-01, same as the rest of the v1 sunset work.

[2026-02-20 09:53] Priya Raghavan
after that date the sha1 header is gone and anything still verifying against it breaks hard.

[2026-02-20 10:04] Dan Osei
should we be telling customers to check the timestamp for replay protection

[2026-02-20 10:06] Priya Raghavan
yes — reject anything where Nametag-Timestamp is more than 5 minutes off their own clock. that's the whole point of moving the timestamp into the signed string. I'd say that's required, not optional, but nobody's written it down anywhere customer-facing.

[2026-02-20 10:07] Dan Osei
noted. filing a docs ticket

[2026-02-20 10:31] Priya Raghavan
also while we're here, the retry numbers floating around are wrong in at least two places. current behavior is 8 attempts over 24 hours with exponential backoff. schedule is roughly:

immediate, +30s, +2m, +10m, +45m, +3h, +8h, +12h

[2026-02-20 10:32] Priya Raghavan
and 410 Gone is special — if the endpoint returns 410 we stop retrying immediately and disable the endpoint. that's intentional, it's how you tell us to stop. any other 4xx we keep retrying.

[2026-02-20 10:35] Dan Osei
is that in the docs

[2026-02-20 10:35] Priya Raghavan
no
